## 2. Texas Driver Handbook (DL-7): copyright and license

- **No open license is stated.** I searched the full DL-7 text (Rev. January 2026, 4,961 extracted lines) for "copyright", "©", "reproduc", "Creative Commons", "all rights", and permission language. None of them appear.
- The cover says only **"This publication is FREE."** That refers to price. It does not grant permission to reproduce the handbook.
- The handbook disclaims that it "is not an official legal reference to Texas traffic laws." It refers readers to the Transportation Code and Penal Code for the actual wording.
- The DPS website footer reads "© 2026 Texas Department of Public Safety" (seen on archived dps.texas.gov pages listed above).
- **Conclusion:** treat DL-7 text and images as **all rights reserved**. Do not copy them. Write facts from the statutes. Handbook URL: https://www.dps.texas.gov/internetforms/forms/dl-7.pdf
- The DPS Class C exams themselves are restricted to schools and may not be shared outside the school (DPS "Section 23" FAQ: https://web.archive.org/web/20251217001800/https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/section-23-request-knowledge-tests-driver-education-schools ; 37 TAC §15.64(a)(2)(B)). **Never reproduce real exam items.**

## 3. Statute facts

Format: **Topic** — fact — *section* — source key (URL in the table above).

### A. Signs & signals

1. **Steady green** — On a circular green light you may go straight or turn left or right unless a sign forbids the turn. You must still yield to vehicles already lawfully in the intersection and to pedestrians in the intersection or an adjacent crosswalk. — *Transp. Code §544.007(b)* — TN544
2. **Green arrow** — On a green arrow you may carefully enter and move only in the arrow's direction. You must yield to pedestrians in an adjacent crosswalk and to other traffic lawfully in the intersection. — *§544.007(c)* — TN544
3. **Steady red** — On a steady red light, stop at the marked stop line. If there is no stop line, stop before the near-side crosswalk. — *§544.007(d)* — TN544
4. **Right on red** — After a full stop on red and yielding to pedestrians and other traffic, you may turn right. You may also turn left from a one-way street onto another one-way street. A posted sign can prohibit either turn. — *§544.007(d), (f)* — TN544
5. **Steady yellow** — A steady yellow light warns that the green movement is ending or that a red light is coming. — *§544.007(e)* — TN544
6. **Dark signal** — If a traffic signal shows no light at all, treat the intersection as having a stop sign. This does not apply to freeway ramp meters or pedestrian hybrid beacons. — *§544.007(i)* — TN544
7. **Flashing red** — Treat a flashing red light like a stop sign. Stop at the stop line, the crosswalk, or the point where you can see cross traffic, then proceed under stop-sign rules. — *§544.008(a)* — TN544
8. **Flashing yellow** — At a flashing yellow light you may proceed, but only with caution. — *§544.008(b)* — TN544
9. **Stop / yield signs** — You must stop at a stop sign. At a yield sign you must stop only if safety requires it. Where to stop: first the marked stop line, then before the crosswalk, then the point nearest the cross road where you can see approaching traffic. — *§544.010* — TN544
10. **Lane-control signals** — You may drive in a lane under a green lane signal. You may not enter or drive in a lane under a red lane signal. — *§544.009* — TN544
11. **Lane-use sign wording** — A sign directing slower traffic out of the far-left lane must read "left lane for passing only." — *§544.011* — TN544
12. **Railroad crossing stop distance** — When a train signal, lowered gate, flagger, or approaching train requires a stop, stop no closer than **15 feet** and no farther than **50 feet** from the nearest rail. — *§545.251(a)* — TN545
13. **Railroad gates** — Driving around, under, or through a crossing gate while it is closed, closing, or opening is an offense. — *§545.251(d)* — TN545
14. **Pedestrian signals** — A pedestrian facing "Walk" may cross, and drivers must stop and yield to them. Pedestrians may not start crossing on "Don't Walk" or "Wait." — *§552.002* — TN552

### B. Right of way

15. **Uncontrolled intersection** — At an intersection with no signs or signals, yield to a vehicle that has entered from your right or is approaching from your right close enough to be a hazard. — *§545.151(d)* — TN545
16. **T-intersection** — If your road ends at an uncontrolled intersection, yield to traffic on the through road. — *§545.151(e)* — TN545
17. **Unpaved to paved road** — A driver entering a paved road from an unpaved road must stop and yield to traffic on the paved road. — *§545.151(c)* — TN545
18. **Left turns** — A driver turning left must yield to oncoming vehicles that are close enough to be an immediate hazard. — *§545.152* — TN545
19. **Yield sign** — At a yield sign, slow to a reasonable speed and yield to traffic close enough to be a hazard. If you drive past a yield sign without stopping and then collide, that is prima facie evidence you failed to yield. — *§545.153(c)–(d)* — TN545
20. **Driveways and alleys** — A driver entering a road from an alley, building, or private driveway must yield to vehicles on that road. — *§545.155* — TN545
21. **Emergency vehicles** — When an emergency vehicle approaches using lights and siren, yield. Pull over parallel to and as close as possible to the right edge or curb, clear of any intersection, and stay stopped until it passes. — *§545.156(a-1)* — TN545
22. **Move over / slow down** — When you approach a stopped emergency vehicle, tow truck, TxDOT or highway work vehicle, utility vehicle, or certain other vehicles showing visual signals:
    - If there are two or more lanes in your direction, move out of the lane next to the vehicle; or
    - Slow to **20 mph below the posted limit**. If the posted limit is under 25 mph, slow to **5 mph**. — *§545.157(a)–(b)* — TN545
23. **Move-over penalty** — Violating the move-over law carries a fine of $500 to $1,250. — *§545.157(c)* — TN545
24. **Stopped school bus** — When a school bus is stopped with its visual signals on to load or unload students, drivers from **either direction** must stop. They may not go until the bus moves, the bus driver signals them on, or the signals turn off. — *§545.066(a)* — TN545
25. **School bus exception** — You need not stop for a school bus on a separate roadway of a divided highway. A center left-turn lane alone does **not** make a separate roadway. — *§545.066(b), (f)* — TN545
26. **Crosswalk yield** — Where there is no working traffic signal, stop and yield to a pedestrian in a crosswalk who is on your half of the road or approaching closely from the other half. — *§552.003(a)* — TN552
27. **Stopped car at crosswalk** — You may not pass a vehicle from behind when it has stopped at a crosswalk to let a pedestrian cross. — *§552.003(c)* — TN552
28. **Driveway and sidewalk** — A driver coming out of or going into an alley, building, or driveway must stop and yield to pedestrians on the sidewalk. — *§552.006(c)* — TN552

### C. Speed

29. **Basic speed rule** — You may not drive faster than is reasonable and prudent for current conditions and hazards. — *§545.351(a)–(b)* — TN545
30. **Slow down when required** — Reduce speed when approaching or crossing an intersection or railroad crossing, approaching or going around a curve, approaching a hill crest, on narrow or winding roads, and when special hazards exist. Hazards include pedestrians, weather, and road conditions. — *§545.351(c)* — TN545
31. **Urban district / alley** — The prima facie limit is **30 mph** on streets in an urban district and **15 mph** in an alley. — *§545.352(b)(1)* — TN545
32. **Numbered highways** — The prima facie limit is **70 mph** on state or U.S. numbered highways outside an urban district, including farm-to-market and ranch-to-market roads. — *§545.352(b)(2)* — TN545
33. **Other rural highways** — The prima facie limit is **60 mph** on highways outside an urban district that are not state or U.S. numbered. — *§545.352(b)(3)* — TN545
34. **Beach** — The speed limit on a beach is **15 mph**. — *§545.352(b)(5)* — TN545
35. **Prima facie meaning** — Driving faster than these limits is prima facie evidence that your speed is unreasonable and unlawful. — *§545.352(a)* — TN545
36. **Higher posted limits** — TxDOT may normally set limits up to 75 mph. It may set 80 mph on parts of I-10/I-20 in certain West Texas counties, and up to **85 mph** on highway sections designed for it. — *§545.353(d), (h-1), (h-2)* — TN545
37. **Day / night limits** — A speed limit set or changed under this subchapter must be the same for day and night. — *§545.352(e)* — TN545
38. **Minimum speed** — You may not drive so slowly that you block the normal flow of traffic, unless slower speed is needed for safety or by law. — *§545.363(a)* — TN545
39. **School zones** — State law sets **no fixed statewide school-zone speed**. Local authorities set those limits, and cities and counties must hold an annual hearing if a school asks them to (§545.357). Texas bans **hand-held phone use** in a school crossing zone unless the vehicle is stopped or hands-free (§545.425(b)). DWI committed in a school crossing zone while its reduced limit is in effect is a **state jail felony** (Penal Code §49.04(e)). — TN545, PE49

### D. Lanes, passing & turning

40. **Turn signal distance** — Signal continuously for at least the **last 100 feet** before turning. — *§545.104(b)* — TN545
41. **When to signal** — Use a signal to turn, change lanes, or pull away from a parked position. Do not use turn signals as a courtesy "go ahead and pass" signal. — *§545.104(a), (c)* — TN545
42. **Hand signals** — Hand signals are given from the left side of the vehicle. **Left turn:** arm straight out. **Right turn:** arm bent upward. **Stop or slow:** arm bent downward. — *§545.107* — TN545
43. **Right turns** — Approach and make a right turn as close as practical to the right curb or edge. — *§545.101(a)* — TN545
44. **Left turns** — Approach a left turn from the far-left lawful lane and finish in a lawful lane for your direction. On a two-way street, turn to the left of the center of the intersection. — *§545.101(b)–(c)* — TN545
45. **Passing** — Pass on the left at a safe distance and do not return to the right until clear of the passed vehicle. If you are being passed, do not speed up until the pass is complete. — *§545.053* — TN545
46. **Return before oncoming traffic** — When passing in an oncoming lane, return to your lane before coming within **200 feet** of an approaching vehicle. — *§545.054(b)* — TN545
47. **No-passing zones** — Do not drive left of center or left of no-passing striping in a no-passing zone. You may cross it to turn left into or out of an alley or driveway. — *§545.055(b)* — TN545
48. **100-ft limits on left of center** — Do not drive on the left side of the road within **100 feet** of an intersection or railroad crossing in a city, or within 100 feet of a bridge, viaduct, or tunnel. — *§545.056(a)* — TN545
49. **Passing on the right** — You may pass on the right only when it is safe and the other vehicle is turning left. The road must also have room for two or more lanes moving in your direction, or be a wide enough one-way street. — *§545.057* — TN545
50. **Improved shoulder** — You may drive on a right-side improved shoulder only when necessary and safe, and only for listed reasons:
    - to stop, stand, or park;
    - to accelerate onto the road or slow down for a right turn;
    - to pass a vehicle that is slowing, stopped, disabled, or turning left;
    - to let faster traffic pass;
    - to avoid a collision. — *§545.058(a)* — TN545
51. **Center lane on 3-lane road** — On a three-lane two-way road, use the center lane only to pass when it is clear, to prepare for a left turn, or where signs assign it to your direction. — *§545.060(b)* — TN545
52. **Following distance** — Keep an assured clear distance so you can stop safely without hitting the vehicle ahead. — *§545.062(a)* — TN545

### E. Parking

53. **Never stop, stand, or park in these places** (*§545.302(a)* — TN545):
    - on a sidewalk;
    - in an intersection;
    - on a crosswalk;
    - on a railroad track;
    - on a bridge or in a highway tunnel;
    - double-parked beside a car already at the curb.
54. **Fire hydrant** — Do not stand or park within **15 feet** of a fire hydrant. — *§545.302(b)(2)* — TN545
55. **Crosswalk** — Do not stand or park within **20 feet** of a crosswalk at an intersection. — *§545.302(b)(3)* — TN545
56. **Signs and signals** — Do not stand or park within **30 feet** on the approach to a flashing signal, stop sign, yield sign, or traffic signal at the side of the road. — *§545.302(b)(4)* — TN545
57. **Fire station** — Do not stand or park within **20 feet** of a fire-station driveway. On the opposite side of the street, the limit is **75 feet** of the entrance, when the entrance is marked with a sign. — *§545.302(b)(5)* — TN545
58. **Driveway** — Do not stand or park in front of a public or private driveway, except briefly to pick up or drop off a passenger. — *§545.302(b)(1)* — TN545
59. **Railroad** — Do not park within **50 feet** of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing, except temporarily to load or unload. — *§545.302(c)(1)* — TN545
60. **Curb distance** — On a two-way road, park parallel with your right wheels within **18 inches** of the right curb or edge. — *§545.303(a)* — TN545
61. **Safety zones** — Do not park between a safety zone and the curb, or within 30 feet of the curb opposite a safety zone's ends, unless the city sets a different length. — *§545.302(a)(5)* — TN545

### F. Alcohol & drugs

62. **Definition of intoxicated** — You are legally intoxicated if alcohol, drugs, or another substance takes away the normal use of your mental or physical faculties, **or** if your alcohol concentration is **0.08 or more**. — *Penal Code §49.01(2)* — PE49
63. **DWI** — Operating a motor vehicle in a public place while intoxicated is an offense. A first offense is a Class B misdemeanor with at least 72 hours in jail. — *Penal Code §49.04(a)–(b)* — PE49
64. **High BAC** — A DWI with an alcohol concentration of **0.15 or more** becomes a Class A misdemeanor. — *Penal Code §49.04(d)* — PE49
65. **DWI with open container** — If the driver had an open container in their immediate possession, the minimum jail term rises to six days. — *Penal Code §49.04(c)* — PE49
66. **Open container** — It is an offense to knowingly have an open container of alcohol in a vehicle's passenger area on a public highway, even if the vehicle is parked. A locked glove box, the trunk, or the area behind the last upright seat does not count as the passenger area. The offense is a Class C misdemeanor. — *Penal Code §49.031* — PE49
67. **Minors: zero tolerance** — A minor commits an offense by driving in a public place with **any detectable amount** of alcohol in their system. — *Alco. Bev. Code §106.041(a)* — AL106
68. **Minor definition** — In the Alcoholic Beverage Code, a "minor" is anyone **under 21**. — *Alco. Bev. Code §106.01* — AL106
69. **Implied consent** — A person arrested for DWI or a minor's DUI is deemed to have consented to breath or blood testing for alcohol or drugs. — *Transp. Code §724.011(a)* — TN724
70. **Refusal** — Refusing the test leads to a **180-day** license suspension. It becomes **2 years** if the person had an alcohol- or drug-related enforcement contact in the previous 10 years. — *Transp. Code §724.035(a)–(b)* — TN724

### G. Safe driving & equipment

71. **Seat belts** — Anyone **15 or older** riding in a seat with a belt must wear it. Fine: $25–$50. — *§545.413(a), (d)* — TN545
72. **Driver's duty for children** — The driver must make sure any child **under 17** who is not required to use a child seat is belted. Fine: $100–$200. — *§545.413(b), (d)* — TN545
73. **Child safety seat** — A child **younger than 8** must ride in a child safety seat system unless the child is **taller than 4 feet 9 inches**. — *§545.412(a)* — TN545
74. **Texting ban** — No driver may read, write, or send electronic messages on a hand-held device unless the vehicle is stopped. Exceptions include hands-free use, GPS navigation, emergencies, and playing music. — *§545.4251(b)–(c)* — TN545
75. **Under-18 phone ban** — Drivers under 18 may not use any wireless communication device, **including hands-free**, except in an emergency. — *§545.424(a), (f)* — TN545
76. **Headlights required** — Lights must be on at nighttime, and whenever people or vehicles cannot be clearly seen at **1,000 feet** ahead. — *§547.302(a)* — TN547
77. **Dimming for oncoming traffic** — Switch to low beams within **500 feet** of an oncoming vehicle. — *§547.333(c)(1)* — TN547
78. **Dimming when following** — Do not use high beams within **300 feet** of a vehicle you are following. — *§547.333(c)(2)* — TN547
79. **Beam reach** — High beams must reveal people or vehicles at least 450 feet ahead. Low beams must reveal them at least 150 feet ahead. — *§547.333(b)* — TN547
80. **Coasting** — Do not coast downhill with the transmission in neutral. — *§545.406(a)* — TN545
81. **Reckless driving** — Driving with willful or wanton disregard for people's or property safety is an offense. The penalty is up to a $200 fine and/or up to 30 days in jail. — *§545.401* — TN545

### H. Licenses, GDL & collisions

82. **License required** — You may not drive on a Texas highway without a driver's license unless an exemption applies. — *§521.021* — TN521
83. **Carry your license** — Carry your license while driving and show it on request to a peace officer, magistrate, or court officer. — *§521.025(a)* — TN521
84. **Learner license** — A learner license is available at ages **15 through 17** after passing the driver-ed classroom phase and the required exams other than the driving test. — *§521.222(a)* — TN521
85. **Learner supervision** — A learner may drive only with the license in their possession. A licensed driver who is **21 or older** with at least **1 year** of driving experience must sit in the front seat beside them. — *§521.222(d)* — TN521
86. **Learner hold period** — Before getting a Class C license, an applicant under 18 must have held a learner or hardship license for at least **6 months**. — *§521.204(b)* — TN521
87. **Minimum age for a provisional license** — An applicant under 18 must be at least **16** to get a Class C license. They also need a driver-ed certificate, school enrollment or a diploma, and must pass the exams. — *§521.204(a)* — TN521
88. **Teen restrictions** — Drivers under 18 may not drive **between midnight and 5 a.m.** unless it is for work, a school activity, or a medical emergency. They may not carry **more than one passenger under 21** who is not a family member. These limits do not apply while accompanied as a learner. — *§545.424(a-1), (c)* — TN545
89. **Address or name change** — Notify DPS within **30 days** of moving or changing your name, and apply for a duplicate license. — *§521.054(b)* — TN521
90. **Injury crash** — A driver in a crash with injury or death must stop, return if not stopped, check whether anyone needs aid, and stay at the scene. — *§550.021(a)* — TN550
91. **Exchange information** — After a crash with injury or a damaged attended vehicle, give your name, address, vehicle registration number, and insurer. Show your license if asked, and give reasonable help to injured people. — *§550.023* — TN550
92. **Freeway fender-bender** — In a metro-area freeway crash where every vehicle can still be driven safely, move the vehicles off the main lanes to a safe nearby location. — *§550.022(b)* — TN550
93. **Unattended vehicle** — If you hit an unattended vehicle, stop. Then either find the owner or leave a written note with your name, address, and an explanation. — *§550.024(a)* — TN550
94. **Immediate police notice** — If a crash causes injury, death, or a vehicle that can't be driven, report it immediately to the police (in a city) or the sheriff or DPS. — *§550.026(a)* — TN550
95. **Officer report threshold** — An investigating officer must file a written report when a crash causes injury, death, or apparent property damage of **$1,000 or more** to any one person. The report must be filed within 10 days. — *§550.062(a)–(b)* — TN550

## Notes / cautions

- **§545.424(a-1)** in its current text applies to *all* drivers under 18, not just the first 12 months of a provisional license. The DPS provisional-license page describes the same passenger, curfew, and phone limits: https://web.archive.org/web/2026/https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/texas-provisional-license-teen
- **§550.062** sets the duty for **officers** to report crashes (the $1,000 threshold). The driver-filed CR-2 "blue form" was not verified here, so do not write questions about it.
- **UNVERIFIED:** the number of questions on the Class C knowledge test (see §1). Every other fact above was confirmed from the primary-source URLs listed.
